1. **Do not open a public issue.**
2. Reach out via the secure channels specified in my profile or via **Substack Private Message**.
3. All findings are handled with a **90-day disclosure window** to allow for mitigation before any public research summary is released.
4. We aim to acknowledge all reports within 48 hours.
